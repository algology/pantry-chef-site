Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026 (Community added)

Cookroo helps you cook with what you already have. We collect as little as we can. There are no ads and no tracking, and you only need an account if you join Community.

Your data stays in your iCloud

Your pantry, recipes, collections, meal plan and shopping list are stored on your device and synced to your other devices through your own iCloud account. Apple stores that data; we can't see it.

If you share your shopping list and meal plan with a household, the people you invite can see and change them, through iCloud. You can stop sharing, or leave a household, at any time in Settings.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Fridge and receipt photos. When you scan, the photo is sent to our server and to our AI provider (OpenAI models, accessed through OpenRouter) to identify the food in it. We don't store your photos. Our providers process them under their own privacy terms and may keep them briefly for abuse monitoring.
  • Pantry list and preferences. When you ask for dinner ideas, the names of your pantry items, your diet choices, foods you dislike and household size are sent the same way to create recipes. We don't store them.
  • App identifier and IP address. Each request includes a random identifier Apple gives this app on your device, plus your IP address. We use these only to apply fair-use limits, as counters that reset within 30 days.
  • Recipe reports. If you report a recipe, we store the recipe, the reason you chose and the app identifier so we can review it.

Community

Community is optional. If you join, you sign in with Apple. We don't store your Apple ID: we keep a scrambled (hashed) version of the identifier Apple gives us, so we can recognise you next time. We store:

  • your display name and when you accepted the Community terms;
  • the recipes and photos you post, which other Cookroo users can see once they're approved;
  • the recipes you like, the cooks you block and the reports you send.

To keep Community safe, recipe text, photos and display names are checked by our moderation service, Paxmod, before they're shown.

You can delete your Community account in the app at any time. That removes your account, your recipes and photos, and your likes.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, use advertising identifiers, or track you across other apps and websites.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete all my data to remove everything stored on your phone. To delete a recipe report or a waitlist email, email us and we'll remove it.

Children

Cookroo isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
