Common questions

The scan missed something or got it wrong

On the "Check what we found" screen you can rename, delete or add items before they're saved. Good light and an open fridge door help.

I bought Cookroo Unlock on another phone

Go to Settings → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

A recipe looks wrong or unsafe

Open the recipe, tap ⋯ → Report this recipe. Recipes are AI-generated, so always check ingredients and labels for allergens.

I hit a limit

To keep costs fair, there's a monthly fair-use limit on scans and dinner ideas. The message tells you when it resets.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all my data. See our privacy policy for details.
