Community Terms

Last updated 1 November 2026

Community is where Cookroo cooks share their own recipes. To keep it friendly and useful, everyone who posts agrees to these terms.

Post only your own recipes and photos

Share recipes you wrote or adapted yourself and photos you took. Don't copy recipes, text or photos from cookbooks, websites or other people.

Keep it kind and safe

  • No offensive, hateful, sexual, violent or harassing content, in recipes, photos or display names.
  • No spam, ads or links, and nothing that isn't a recipe.
  • No recipes that are dangerous to follow.

How we check posts

New recipes and photos are checked automatically before they appear. Some are held for a person to review first. Recipes are shared as-is by other cooks, so always check ingredients and labels for allergens.

Reporting and blocking

If something breaks these terms, tap Report on the recipe. Reported recipes are reviewed, and recipes with several reports are hidden until we've looked. You can also block a cook to stop seeing their recipes.

What we can do

We may remove any content, and suspend or ban any account, that breaks these terms or that we believe harms the community. We'll act on reports of objectionable content as quickly as we can.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you let us show it to other Cookroo users in the app and on our website. You can delete a recipe, or your whole Community account, at any time in the app.

Changes

If these terms change, we'll update this page and the date above.
